Tuner not working? Microphone and reading problems solved

A tuner in a web page depends on three things: the browser's permission to use the microphone, a microphone that can hear your instrument, and a clean, single note. When something goes wrong, it is nearly always one of these. Work through the list in order.

"The microphone was not allowed"

When you tap Start, the browser asks whether this site may use the microphone. If you tapped "Block" or "Don't allow", or dismissed the question, the tuner cannot listen, and the browser will usually not ask again by itself.

  • Chrome on Android or a computer: tap the small icon at the left end of the address bar (a padlock or a "tune" symbol), open Permissions or Site settings, and set Microphone to Allow. Reload the page.
  • Safari on iPhone or iPad: tap the aA or page-settings button in the address bar, choose Website Settings, and set Microphone to Allow or Ask. Also check the phone's Settings app, under Safari, that Microphone is not set to Deny.
  • Firefox: click the padlock in the address bar and clear the blocked microphone permission, then reload.
  • On any phone, the browser app itself needs the microphone permission from the system. In the phone's settings, find the browser under Apps and allow Microphone.

Menus are renamed from time to time, so the wording on your device may differ a little; look for "Microphone" under the site's or the app's permissions.

"No microphone was found"

  • Desktop computers often have no built-in microphone. Plug in a headset or a webcam.
  • On a laptop, check that the microphone is not switched off by a key or a privacy switch.
  • If several microphones are connected, the browser may be using the wrong one; choose the right one in the browser's site settings.

The tuner listens but shows nothing

  • Too quiet or too far. Bring the device within arm's length of the instrument, and play a little more firmly.
  • Another app is using the microphone. A call, a voice recorder or a video meeting can hold it. Close them.
  • In-app browsers. Pages opened inside a chat or social app sometimes cannot use the microphone. Open the page in your phone's ordinary browser.
  • The page is not secure. Browsers allow the microphone only on addresses that begin with https. This site always does.

The needle jumps about

  • Background noise. Fans, coolers, traffic, a television and other people talking all reach the microphone. A ceiling fan is a frequent culprit in Indian homes: its hum is a low, steady note of its own. Move to a quieter room or switch it off for a minute.
  • More than one string ringing. Play a single string and rest your hand on the others.
  • Plucking too hard. A hard pluck is sharp at first and rattles. Pluck gently and read the needle after a second.
  • Very low notes. Small microphones hear bass poorly. Play the 12th-fret harmonic instead, which is an octave higher, or bring the device close to the amplifier.
  • Dead strings. Old strings give an unsteady note that no tuner can read well.

It shows the wrong note

  • The string is far out. The tuner shows the nearest note, which may not be the one you want. Play the reference tone for the string and get close by ear first.
  • An overtone is being heard. If the display flickers between two notes, an octave or a fifth apart, pluck more softly nearer the middle of the string.
  • You have set swara names. If the display says "Pa" instead of "G", a Sa has been chosen on the find your Sa page. Set "Show notes as" back to letter names there.
  • The reference pitch was changed. Check that A is set to 440 on the home page tuner.

The reference tones are silent

  • Turn the media volume up, and check that the phone is not in silent mode; some phones mute web pages with the ringer switch.
  • Very low tones, such as those of a bass, are below what small speakers can play. Use earphones.
  • Bluetooth speakers add a short delay but play the correct pitch.

Does the tuner record me?

No. The sound from the microphone is analysed inside your browser a few times a second and discarded at once. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent to us or anyone else. While the tuner is listening, your browser shows its microphone indicator; when you tap Stop, close the tab or switch to another app, the microphone is released and the indicator goes out.
