How to use the tuner

  1. Tap Start tuner. Your browser asks for permission to use the microphone; allow it.
  2. Play one note and let it ring.
  3. The large letter is the nearest note. The needle shows how far you are from it: left of centre is too low (flat), right is too high (sharp).
  4. Adjust your instrument until the needle rests in the middle and the display says "In tune".
  5. Tap Stop when you finish. The microphone is released at once.

This is a chromatic tuner: it names whichever of the twelve notes is nearest, so it works for any instrument and any tuning. If you play one of the instruments below, its own page is easier, because it knows which strings to expect and plays a reference tone for each.

Your privacy

The microphone is used only after you tap Start, and only to measure pitch. The sound is analysed inside your browser and discarded immediately. It is never recorded, saved or sent to us or to anyone else. You can see this for yourself: your browser's microphone indicator is lit while the tuner listens and goes out when you stop.

Getting a good reading

  • Tune in a quiet place. Fans, coolers and televisions confuse any tuner.
  • Play one string at a time, and rest a hand on the others.
  • Pluck gently and read the needle after a second, when the note has settled.
  • Tune up to a note: if you are sharp, go below and come back up.
  • Go round all the strings twice.
