Tuner: questions and answers

Short answers to common questions. If the tuner is not hearing you, go straight to tuner not working.

Is this tuner free?

Yes. It is free, needs no app and no account, and works in the browser of a phone, tablet or computer.

Does it record or send my sound?

No. The microphone is opened only when you tap Start. The sound is analysed in your browser and thrown away at once. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and Stop releases the microphone.

How accurate is it?

In our tests with computer-made string tones from the lowest bass B to high notes above the violin's range, the pitch detector was within one cent every time. With a real microphone in a real room, expect a few cents, which is as close as most ears can hear. Noise and very low notes reduce accuracy.

What does "cents" mean?

A cent is one hundredth of a semitone, the step between two neighbouring frets or keys. A reading of minus 10 cents means the note is a tenth of a semitone too low.

Which instruments can I tune?

Any instrument that plays one clear note at a time: guitar, bass, ukulele, violin, viola, cello, mandolin, banjo, tanpura, sitar strings, wind instruments and the voice. Dedicated pages list the strings of eight instruments; the home page tuner names any note.

Why does the tuner show a different string from the one I played?

It shows the nearest note. A string that is far out of tune can be nearer to its neighbour. Play the reference tone for the string and bring it close by ear first.

Can I change the reference from A = 440?

Yes, on the home page tuner, between 415 and 466 hertz. Leave it at 440 unless you are playing with others who use a different pitch.

Can it show Sa Re Ga Ma?

Yes. On the "Find your Sa" page choose the key of your Sa, and the tuner and tone generator name notes as swaras.

Why does the tuner struggle with my bass or cello?

Phone and laptop microphones are weak at very low notes. Let the note ring, bring the device closer, or play the octave harmonic at the 12th fret or the midpoint of the string.

It says the microphone was not allowed. What now?

Allow the microphone for this site in your browser's site settings and reload the page. The troubleshooting page gives the steps for Android, iPhone and computers.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to open the page. The tuning itself is done on your device.
