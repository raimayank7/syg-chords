How to use the chord finder

  1. Choose a root note, such as C or F♯.
  2. Choose a chord type, such as minor or 7.
  3. The chord page shows the chord on guitar, ukulele and keyboard, its notes and formula, a button to hear it, the keys it belongs to and chords that go well with it.

What is on each chord page

  • Guitar charts: the open shape where one exists, and movable barre shapes, each with a description in words.
  • Ukulele chart for standard G, C, E, A tuning.
  • Keyboard chart with the notes marked, and the inversions listed.
  • The notes and the formula, so that you learn how the chord is built and not just where to put your fingers.
  • Where it fits: the keys that contain the chord and common movements to and from it.

Every guitar and ukulele fingering on the site is checked by program to contain exactly the notes of its chord.
