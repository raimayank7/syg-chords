Chords: questions and answers

Short answers to common questions about the charts and the site. To learn to read the charts, see how to read chord diagrams.

How many chords are on this site?

There are 144 chords: twelve types (major, minor, seventh, major seventh, minor seventh, sus2, sus4, sixth, added ninth, diminished, augmented and power chord) on each of the twelve roots. Every one has a guitar chart, a ukulele chart and a keyboard chart.

Are the chord charts correct?

Every guitar and ukulele fingering is checked by program when the site is built: the notes produced by the fretted and open strings must be exactly the notes of the chord. In a few four-note chords the common guitar shape leaves out the fifth, and the page says so.

Do you have chords or lyrics for songs?

No. This site teaches chords themselves: shapes, notes and how they fit together. It contains no song lyrics, tabs or song chord sheets.

What does the short code like x 3 2 0 1 0 mean?

It lists the fret for each string from the thickest to the thinnest. x means do not play the string and 0 means play it open.

Why is there more than one way to play a chord?

The same notes can be found in several places on the neck. Open shapes are easiest; barre shapes work for every root. Choose the one nearest to the chords before and after it.

Is C sharp the same as D flat?

On guitar, ukulele and keyboard they are the same pitch, and the chord shapes are identical. Which name is used depends on the key of the music.

What is the easiest chord to start with?

On guitar, E minor: two fingers and all six strings. On ukulele, C major: one finger.

What is a barre chord?

A chord in which the first finger presses several strings at one fret, so that a shape can be moved up the neck to any root. See the barre chords guide.

Does the "Hear this chord" button use real instrument sounds?

It plays the notes of the chord with a simple tone made by your browser, first one after another and then together. It is for checking the notes by ear, not an imitation of a guitar.

How do I play a song in a different key?

Move every chord by the same number of semitones, or use a capo. The capo and transposing page has a tool and a chart for this.

Do the chords work for left-handed players?

Yes. On a left-handed instrument strung in reverse, read each diagram as a mirror image.

Can I print the chord charts?

Yes, use your browser's print command on any chord page. You may print charts for your own practice or your class.
